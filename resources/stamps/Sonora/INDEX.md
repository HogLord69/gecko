# Sonora

Stamps in Sonora's numbers, laid out by tools/organize_stamps.py: Buildings by what they are built from, Graveyards, Junkyards, Ruins. Size: buildings by roofed squares (Small under 60, Medium under 150, Large under 400, Huge), ruins by the hexes their pieces stand on (Small under 30, Medium under 50, Large). A stamp named with a 2nd or 3rd floor is a building on that elevation of its map: stamp it on that elevation (it may be an upper storey or an area of its own).
