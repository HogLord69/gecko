# Resurrection

Stamps in Resurrection's numbers, laid out by tools/organize_stamps.py: Buildings by construction and size (roofed squares: Small under 60, Medium under 150, Large under 400, Huge), Ruins by size (hexes with objects: Small under 30, Medium under 50, Large), Graveyards, Junkyards. A stamp named with a 2nd or 3rd floor is a building on that elevation of its map: stamp it on that elevation (it may be an upper storey or an area of its own).
